Vote of No Confidence from Jasmin Movement Shattered in the Parliament

A motion of no confidence requested by George Miliband of the Jasmin Movement was swiftly dismantled on the floor today. Parliament, including members of the opposition, voiced significant support for Prime Minister Patrick Cutter, likely citing the critical need for stability during the ongoing Myratian Crisis. Further complicating the motion, the Jasmin Movement itself appeared fractured; party chairman Jasmin was observed voting in favor of the government, severely undermining her own party's maneuver.

By Elsa Rourke, AVIS Politics Desk 28 March 2058, 20:45

City Council Declares War on Potholes; Potholes Respond With Aggressive Expansion

Following a bold declaration by the Mayor to eradicate all potholes by the end of the fiscal year, local asphalt anomalies seem to have taken the threat personally. A rapid overnight expansion on 4th Avenue has effectively consumed a small sedan. The Mayor's office insists the situation is under control, urging citizens to simply "drive better."

By Nadia Voss, AVIS Society Desk 28 March 2058, 09:15

Local Man Discovers He's Been Paying Rent to a Pigeon for Three Years

A startling revelation emerged in a downtown apartment complex today when a resident learned his elusive landlord, "Mr. Coo," was in fact a particularly enterprising rock dove nesting in the building's ventilation shaft. Legal experts remain divided on whether the pigeon is required to return the security deposit.
