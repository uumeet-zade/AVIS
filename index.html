AVIS FIELD REPORT: Myrati - A Fragile Turning Point

After weeks of grinding, inconclusive engagements, the rural theater in Myrati has finally produced a tangible shift in momentum. Ex-ODU troops successfully located and destroyed a major separatist ammunition cache. Meanwhile, a mass-casualty attack by the MLF on the Collective Nightclub in Central Montiablo has claimed 13 lives, including eight CIVIC employees. In the southern waters, an extensive smuggling and human trafficking network linked to Los Navegantes Del Mar has been uncovered, raising fears of connections to the MLF.

By Elsa Rourke, AVIS Politics Desk 9 April 2058, 10:00

Mandy Trottier's Scandalous Coffee Order

President Mandy Trottier's preferred drink, according to sources close to the Presidential residence, is a Pumpkin Spice Sweet Cold Coffee - extra cinnamon, cold foam, no exceptions. The detail has drawn quiet notice in circles that expect a certain austerity from the office. Trottier's public rhetoric is firmly Center-Left: talk of worker-class, labour, the sacrifices we must make for the Republic. Her order suggests other priorities. Aides describe the order as a fixture of her schedule, not an occasional indulgence. One source, speaking on condition of anonymity, noted it appears on the daily brief alongside her security detail's rotation. AVIS reached out to the President's office for comment and received none.

By AVIS Society Desk 10 April 2058, 08:30

MP von Reichel Ties the Knot With Senator Proctor in Ceremony with Higher Attendance than the Parliament

Member of Parliament and AVIS founder Aldric von Reichel officially became Aldric von Reichel and Senator Mary Proctor became Mary Proctor Reichel today, in a ceremony that reportedly drew more sitting legislators than most actual parliamentary sessions this year. Guests included several high-ranking government officials, numerous MPs, and a healthy contingent of Social Democratic Alliance members, leading one attendee to joke that "if this venue collapsed, we'd need an emergency election."

By Nadia Voss, AVIS Society Desk 9 April 2058, 18:40
