Public Housing Waitlists Grow as Applications Outpace New Construction

Waitlists for public housing have lengthened for the third consecutive quarter, according to figures released by the housing authority, as applications continue to outpace the rate of new unit construction. Housing officials attribute the gap to rising demand; advocacy groups point instead to construction approvals delayed at the municipal level.
