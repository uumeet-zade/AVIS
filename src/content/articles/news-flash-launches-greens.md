From their headquarters at 110 Acton Circle, Montiablo, the steering committee of Caprica's newest political force has officially planted its flag. As Ecologica launches its inaugural issue today, we look back at the Democratic Greens of Caprica (DGC), which formally emerged on July 12, 2065, ending weeks of speculation following the fracture within Progressive Caprica.

At the helm is Adriana Flash, the 19th Prime Minister of Caprica, flanked by party chair Aster Skye and senior counsel Henrik Vasmer. Their founding document left no room for ambiguity: the DGC intends to govern.

"Sustainability is not a special interest—it is a governance-wide duty," Flash remarked to a packed room of supporters and press during the launch last year. "We are extending our mandate beyond environmental policy into fiscal, infrastructure, and social policy. A better world is possible, but we must have the courage to build it."

The party's platform leans heavily into Georgist land value taxes, sweeping social liberalism, and a market-based approach to environmentalist goals. A sharp contrast to the more traditional left-wing environmental movements seen globally. 

With Flash already holding her seat in Costa Blanca, the Democratic Greens entered Parliament as a seasoned, immediate player in Caprican politics.
