Former Caprican President Mandy Trottier has been located alive at a ranch near Cosne-d'Allier following a catastrophic explosion that tore through her security detail in Lusignon. While Trottier survived the assassination attempt alongside six security agents, she sustained severe shrapnel wounds that have left her permanently blinded in her left eye. 

The blast claimed the lives of four Caprican security agents positioned in the trailing vehicle, which bore the full brunt of the explosion. 

Trottier had been dispatched to Norwyn under a strict seal of secrecy to negotiate directly with the Norwynian government. The clandestine nature of her visit collapsed immediately upon confirmation of the attack, forcing both Caprican and Norwynian officials to acknowledge her presence. 

Caprican intelligence apparatus COIN successfully ascertained Trottier's location in the aftermath. Although her detail explicitly requested a COIN extraction escort, Trottier was unexpectedly airlifted to a Columbian Union base in Maraiselle by a Norwynian military helicopter under the direct orders of President Beauregard. 

Responsibility for the attack has been claimed by the Rochelist terrorist faction *la Front pour une Norwyn Libérée*. In a video released shortly after the bombing, the group characterized Trottier as an "agent of evil from the disgusting Columbian axis." 

The diplomatic fallout remains severe. President Beauregard issued a swift, public statement offering "the strongest of apologies" to Trottier and the Caprican state specifically regarding this incident. He further pledged a comprehensive crackdown on the operations of illicit militant groups operating within Norwynian borders. 

Investigations into how the Rochelist cell acquired the heavily guarded itinerary of a former Caprican head of state remain ongoing.
