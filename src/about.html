About AVIS

Agence pour la Veille et l'Information Sociale (AVIS) is a dedicated news organization committed to acting as the Record for the People.

Founded: March 28, 2058

Founder: Aldric von Reichel
Sitting Member of Parliament, Social Democratic Alliance

Operating independently, AVIS focuses exclusively on Politics, Insider reports, and Society news. We strive to provide transparent, unvarnished insight into the legislative process, behind-closed-doors negotiations, and the day-to-day realities of life in Caprica.
