LEAKED: The 4.2 Million Argent 'Tactical Horse' Deployed to Myrati

By AVIS Insider Desk 6 October 2058, 08:15

Documents obtained exclusively by AVIS reveal a highly classified, and bafflingly expensive, Ministry of National Defense initiative amid the ongoing Myratian Crisis. Deep within the supplemental budget for Ex-ODU forces currently fighting Edelsteinist insurgents, a line item simply titled "Asset Procurement: Equine" accounts for a staggering 4.2 million Argents.

AVIS can confirm that this asset is, in fact, a horse.

According to internal memos from the logistics division, the animal was procured from a private breeder with known, direct connections to President Mandy Trottier. The documentation outlines no specific combat utility, surveillance capabilities, or logistical function for the horse. The only justification provided in the procurement filing is a handwritten note stating that the animal possesses a "very serious, tactical demeanor."

When pressed on the nature of the President's connection to the procurement, one exhausted presidential aide - speaking under strict anonymity - declined to comment on the funding, but noted that President Trottier has lately "shown signs of being a horse."

When reached for official comment, a spokesperson for the Ministry of National Defense initially denied the existence of the animal. Upon being presented with the signed authorization forms, the spokesperson pivoted, claiming the horse was a "vital component of rural theater mobility," before abruptly ending the call.

Military sources stationed in Myrati, however, paint a different picture. "It just stands there," one Ex-ODU officer told AVIS, speaking on the condition of anonymity. "They built a reinforced, climate-controlled stable for it near the command center. We're fighting MLF insurgents in the mud, and this horse gets premium oats shipped in twice a week via armored convoy. Honestly, I think President Mandy Trottier just likes horses."

The revelation comes at a time when Caprica's government is running a deficit of 343.6 billion Argents and the national credit rating has plummeted. While the Cutter administration preaches fiscal responsibility and institutional stability, it appears 4.2 million Argents is not too steep a price for a single, serious-looking horse.
