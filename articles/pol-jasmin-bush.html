Prime Minister Announces Disappearance of Justice Balls and MP Balls; JM Leader Found Amid Bizarre Kidnapping Claims

By Elsa Rourke, AVIS Politics Desk 29 March 2058, 14:30

Prime Minister Patrick Cutter shocked the nation today with the announcement that three prominent political figures—Supreme Court Justice Hugh Balls, Member of Parliament Richard Balls, and Jasmin Movement leader Jasmin Bergvall—had vanished without a trace. The disappearances triggered an immediate nationwide search by CIVIC.

While the whereabouts of Justice Balls and MP Balls remain unknown, the situation took a surreal turn when investigators located Jasmin Bergvall. Officials confirmed that the JM (Jasmin Movement) leader had simply taken an unannounced trip around Moeras island without notifying the government.

However, Bergvall’s return was accompanied by explosive and bizarre accusations. Taking to the social media platform 'Caprik,' the JM leader posted: "AL BUSH wanted to EAT ME, so he KIDNAPPED ME and LOCKED ME in his BASEMENT. I was trapped there until I MIRACULOUSLY managed to ESCAPE!!!"

The target of these claims, AL Bush, is a Member of Parliament for the governing Moderate Reform party and currently serves as the Director of CIVIC—the very agency tasked with investigating the disappearances. Both government officials and the general public have broadly dismissed Bergvall's accusations as "ridiculous," prompting quiet, speculations in political circles regarding the JM leader's current mental well-being.

Despite the safe recovery of the JM leader, serious concerns remain for Justice Hugh Balls and MP Richard Balls. The disappearance of Justice Balls is particularly alarming given his widely documented close relationship with the Edelsteinists and Dame Rachel Edelstein-Powell. With the Myratian Crisis ongoing and Edelsteinist supporters still in armed revolt following Powell's recent conviction for provocation of violence, investigators are racing to determine if the missing Justice is connected to the insurrection.
