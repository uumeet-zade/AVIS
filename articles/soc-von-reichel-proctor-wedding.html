MP von Reichel Ties the Knot With Senator Proctor in Ceremony with Higher Attendance than the Parliament

By Nadia Voss, AVIS Society Desk 9 April 2058, 18:40

Member of Parliament and AVIS founder Aldric von Reichel officially became Aldric von Reichel and Senator Mary Proctor became Mary Proctor Reichel today, in a ceremony that reportedly drew more sitting legislators than most actual parliamentary sessions this year. Guests included several high-ranking government officials, numerous MPs, and a healthy contingent of Social Democratic Alliance members, leading one attendee to joke that "if this venue collapsed, we'd need an emergency election."

Von Reichel, 23, and Proctor, 43 and a sitting Senator with the Alternative Labour Party, represent something of a rare cross-party union - proof, guests noted, that bipartisanship is alive and well, at least at the altar. The nearly two-decade age gap drew a few knowing smiles among attendees, though most agreed the couple seemed entirely unbothered by the math.

The reception ran on a strict Bring Your Own Alcohol policy, a decision guests either praised as refreshingly frugal or quietly blamed for the noticeably uneven quality of toasts as the evening wore on.

Notably absent from the guest list was Proctor's son, who - being only three years younger than his new stepfather - reportedly decided that attending would raise more questions than the marriage itself already had.

Neither party has commented on whether the wedding registry included a coalition agreement.
