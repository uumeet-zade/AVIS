Growth Looks Good on Paper: A Look In the Details of 2057 Economic Report

The government's 2057 economic release tells two different stories. Caprica grew, with GDP up 4.1% to 5.286 trillion Argents. However, spending outpaced revenue, resulting in a deficit of 6.5% of GDP financed entirely through borrowing. This pushed debt-to-GDP to 90.31% and led rating agencies to hold Caprica's credit rating at BBB+ (stable)-the lowest rating in 57 years.
